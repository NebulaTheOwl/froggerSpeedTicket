Welcome to Frogger: Speed Ticket!

Overview

This is a recreation of the classic video game darling Frogger! It's been build from the ground up to play similar to the original retro installment, with one difference; it gets more difficult as you progress. Unlike the original's four differing levels, this variation speeds up with every level you beat! The controls for this game are as simple as they get. Just use the arrow keys to move the frog up, down, left, and right.

Your goal in Frogger is to bring all five frogs to the five slots located at the end of the river before time runs out, guiding them through the dangers of the roads and waters. Score is the name of the game here. Just progressing earns you points, with extra points received for grabbing flies. At the end of each level, you are refilled back to three lives. If you managed to beat the level without even losing a life, you earn an extra life! You can have up to 15 lives at a time. Once you use the last of your lives, its game over. your final score will be displayed, along with the level number you lost at. take a photo to share your results with others, or try to beat it in the next run!

Meet The Gang!

Frogs

These little guys need to get to the other end of the screen, and its your job to help them. despite their species usual nature, these frogs can't swim. cross the road and jump on passing logs and turtles to reach the end!

Rushing Cars





These drivers are in a rush to get where they are going! be careful, they will not slow down, so you need to maneuver around them! The white cars are late for something, so they are moving faster than everyone else, while the white truck is slower than the others to keep it's cargo safe. Duck and weave through the traffic to reach the river!

Turtles

These little guys travel in groups upstream, and act as platforms for the frogs to cross the river on. Very helpful indeed! They are happy to give the frogs a lift, but be careful not to drift off of the screen!

Flies

These bugs hang around in the slots at the other end of the river, constantly changing spots. If you manage to grab one, you get extra points. Chow time!
